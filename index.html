How to use this app

A complete step-by-step guide. You can also download it as a PDF.

Download full PDF guide

1. Getting started

  • Open the app. A splash screen appears for a moment, then the app opens.
  • Create an account with your full name, email and a password of at least 4 characters.
  • If you already have an account, enter the same email and password to log in. A wrong password is rejected.
  • Your data is saved on this device only.

2. Setting up your profile (4 steps)

  • Step 1, Interests: choose from the list (Gym & walks, Reading, Art, Sports, Coding, Music) or type your own interest and press Add.
  • Step 2, Goal: choose one or more goals (Focus on studies, Sleep on time, Stay fit, Scroll less, Learn a new skill) or write your own.
  • Step 3, Screen time: enter your total phone hours per day now and the hours per day you want. You can also add optional hours for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Games.
  • Step 4, Sleep routine: enter your bedtime and wake-up time. The app uses these to calculate your free time and your wind-down time (45 minutes before bed).
  • Press Next to move forward and Back to correct an earlier step.

3. Dashboard (home page)

  • Stats cards at the top show tasks today, completed tasks, your XP points and focus minutes.
  • Today's schedule shows your tasks in time order with a progress bar.
  • Free time card shows the gaps between your tasks, between your wake-up time and your wind-down time. Gaps shorter than 20 minutes are not shown.
  • Screen time card shows your logged hours for today against your target. Enter today's total hours and press Save to update it.

4. Adding a task

  • Type what you will do in the 'What will you do?' box.
  • Choose a start time. This is required and sets your alarm.
  • Choose an end time (optional, but needed for the 'Did you finish?' question).
  • Press Add. The task appears in today's schedule.
  • Tick the box when you finish a task to get 10 XP. Untick it to remove the XP. Press the X to delete a task.

5. Alarms and notifications

  • At a task's start time an alarm sounds and a popup appears.
  • Snooze 5 min: the alarm rings again after 5 minutes.
  • Stop and start: the alarm stops and you begin the task.
  • At a task's end time the app asks 'Did you finish?'. Choose Yes to complete the task and get 10 XP. Choose No to add 30 minutes to the end time; the question returns when that time passes.
  • When you are free between tasks, the popup offers you a choice to add a task, see activities or take a break.
  • To get notifications when the app is in the background, open Profile and press Enable notifications, then allow notifications in your browser.
  • Alarms work only while the app is open in your browser. Keep the browser open, but you can use other tabs.

6. Motivation popup

  • Each time you open the app, a motivation popup appears. Press Let's go! to close it.

7. Activities

  • Open the Activities tab to see real-world activities for your interests.
  • Use the filter buttons at the top to show one interest at a time.
  • Press Add to my schedule to put an activity into today's schedule. Then choose a start time and press Add.

8. Focus timer

  • Open the Focus timer tab and choose 15, 25 or 45 minutes.
  • Press Start to begin, Pause to stop for a moment, and Reset to start over.
  • When a session finishes you earn XP equal to its minutes, and the time is added to your focus minutes for today.

9. Reports

  • The Reports tab shows today's report and the last 7 days.
  • A daily report popup appears at 9 PM each day.
  • A weekly report popup appears every Sunday at 9 PM.
  • Reports show tasks completed, focus time, screen time and days within your screen target.

10. Help and privacy

  • The Help tab has the campus counselor number and helplines.
  • This app is not a doctor or counselor. If you feel overwhelmed or have thoughts of hurting yourself, talk to a trusted person or emergency service right away.
  • Your data stays on this device. It is not shared with parents, teachers or staff.
  • To delete everything, open the Help tab and press Delete my data.

11. Profile, editing and logging out

  • The Profile tab shows your name, bedtime, wake-up time, screen target, interests and goals.
  • Press Edit preferences to change your interests, goals, screen time and sleep times.
  • Press Log out in the sidebar or on the Profile page to sign out. You will see the account screen next time.

12. Tips and troubleshooting

  • Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge for the best results.
  • If a popup does not appear, check that the app is open and that you have not clicked Not now on notifications.
  • If the alarm sound is silent, click anywhere on the page once; browsers need one click before playing sound.
  • If you see the account screen again after closing the app, that is expected. Log in with the same email and password.
  • If something does not work, press F12, open the Console tab and note the red error message.
